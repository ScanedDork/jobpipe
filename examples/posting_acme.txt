Customer Success Manager (Mid-level) — Acme Payments, Prague

Acme Payments builds invoicing and payment software for small and mid-sized businesses across
Central Europe. We are growing our Prague hub and need a Customer Success Manager to own a book
of mid-market accounts in the DACH region.

What you will do
- Own onboarding, adoption and renewals for a portfolio of 40-60 mid-market accounts
- Run quarterly business reviews (QBRs) and build account plans with key stakeholders
- Identify upselling and cross-selling opportunities together with our Account Executives
- Track health scores, NPS and churn risk in Gainsight and Salesforce
- Feed customer insights back to Product Management via Jira

What you bring
- 3+ years in customer success or account management in B2B SaaS
- Proven track record of reducing churn and growing net revenue retention
- Hands-on experience with Salesforce or HubSpot CRM
- Strong negotiation and stakeholder management skills
- English (C1) and German (B2 or higher)
- Data-driven: comfortable with Excel and KPI dashboards

Nice to have
- Experience with Gainsight
- Czech language skills
- Background in fintech or payments
